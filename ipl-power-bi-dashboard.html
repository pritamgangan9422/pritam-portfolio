Project objectives

  • Compare IPL team performance across seasons.
  • Analyze top run scorers and wicket takers.
  • Explore match results, toss decisions and winning trends.
  • Compare venue-wise and season-wise performance.
  • Present useful insights through interactive Power BI visuals.
